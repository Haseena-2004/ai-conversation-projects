How it works

Metrics: pass rate vs expected behaviour, hallucination (invented price, discount, warranty, policy), latency per turn.
v1 has no business facts or guardrails (baseline). v2 adds facts, one-question-at-a-time, no-invention rules, emergency escalation and injection resistance.
Cost is an estimate from text length at $3/$15 per million input/output tokens. Failure types: hallucination, missed emergency, wrong tone, incomplete info, guardrail breach.
Limits: the judge is itself an LLM and can be wrong, so spot-check its reasons. Next steps: v3 from remaining failures, retrieval for policies, voice layer (Vapi/Retell), cost per call.
